Shared system · The Register

Projects are entries, not cards. A real table: index, subject, surface, state, year. Density is the flex — eleven projects scannable in one screen.

State is honest and enumerated. SHIPPED / IN SERVICE / SUNSET / CONCEPT / SPIKE. Failures render at the same weight as wins.

Handoff delta — every entry carries designed 100% · implemented 60%. Your positioning as a per-project measurement rather than a claim.

A live margin rail. Persistent mono scale at the left edge — ticks, current entry, current section — updating with scroll. Navigation that is also the thesis.

Hero = numbered clauses. 01 / 02 / 03, written like a spec. No greeting, no floating shapes.

The risk: no cards, no shadows, no rounded corners anywhere — depth comes only from paper→raised and 1px hairlines — and exactly one accent instance per viewport, enforced as a lint rule in the token layer.
